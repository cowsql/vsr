# Regression tests

Reserved for focused reproductions of actual fixed bugs. Name tests after the
behavior, reference the issue or invariant, and retain minimized fault traces
or fuzz inputs beside the reproducer. Register executable regressions in
`check_PROGRAMS`/`TESTS` so they run under `make check` and sanitizers.

| Test | Origin | Behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `trim_resend` | `tests/fuzzy/cluster 1017 1 2000 quiet 0` | A primary whose readable revision holds a not-yet-durable TRIM must not load or resend the compacted prefix to a lagging peer; the peer is steered to state transfer (checkpoint FETCH on a full replica, anchor only on a witness) and the cluster converges |
| `recovery_coverage` | `tests/fuzzy/cluster 108 1 2000 quiet 1` | A witness recovering from a lost store must install the primary's remote anchor without waiting for f+1 retention advertisements (which the transition module also dropped whenever they carried a later view), rejoin, and follow a later view change |
| `transfer_anchor` | `tests/fuzzy/cluster 8 1 2000 quiet 5` (also 62, 190) | While a witness compares its retained log against a new primary's untrimmed history, coverage advertisements must not publish a remote anchor and trim under the transfer's cursor; the pending comparison LOADs still find their entries, and the anchor is adopted afterwards |
| `witness_prefix` | `tests/fuzzy/cluster 273 1 2000 quiet 4` (also profile 5) | A witness holding the very entry a donor's anchor closes with keeps its prefix instead of restoring the anchor, so a lost full member can recover through it rather than deadlocking on promises the recovering member cannot yet make |
| `boundary_from_view_change` | `tests/fuzzy/cluster 4 1 470 quiet 32` (also seeds 22, 42, 81, 95, 115, 124, 136, 160, 174, 188 at 600 steps) | A RECONFIGURE boundary learned as committed from VIEW_CHANGE (the old primary's deferred commit store, or a new primary's log selection fed by a witness offer) must abandon the old-epoch round and install directly, promise EPOCH_STARTED, and let the group reach STEADY instead of electing forever in the new epoch |
| `review_epoch_recovery_vote` | protocol safety review (fixed) | A full member that lost its store must not rejoin as a voter without the recovery quorum. Its RECOVERY carries the seed epoch, a survivor answers NEW_EPOCH, and the old core let the epoch handoff install one donor's lagging history and enter NORMAL, so the two survivors elected a view whose log lacked commands the unreachable primary had acknowledged. A replica that owes a quorum recovery now restarts it in the learned epoch (`vsr_transition_epoch_recover` from the handoff's FENCE stage) against that epoch's membership and quorum; the member stays RECOVERING until the primary returns, then converges on the acknowledged history |
| `learner_restart` | `tests/fuzzy/cluster 1 1 600 quiet 125` (also 3, and `3 1 600 quiet 120`) | A JOIN learner restarted with RECOVER resumes nonvoting warm-up instead of entering quorum recovery as a non-member, whether its durable store survived, was lost before the first SYNC, or is replicated; it never persists a role its validator rejects, survives two restarts, and is then admitted and executes commands |
| `learner_follows` | idle-learner observation of the seeded scheduler | Warm-up is continuous: an idle full or witness learner keeps up with later commits, follows a later epoch it is not named in to STEADY, and is admitted with only the remaining suffix to transfer, never voting on the way |
| `epoch_announcement_recovery` | `tests/fuzzy/cluster 108 1 600 quiet 127` (fixed) | A replicated-mode member that lost its store and is RECOVERING must not be pulled out of quorum recovery by a START_EPOCH announcement: the old core's handoff abandoned the recovery round and installed one donor's history, so the member reported TRANSITIONING with a committed position below the one it acknowledged before the crash. Same mechanism as `review_epoch_recovery_vote`, reached through the announcement instead of the NEW_EPOCH redirect; both entry points of `vsr_epochs_event` (the pending fence and the direct `begin`) now lead to the FENCE stage, where the owed quorum recovery restarts in the learned epoch |
| `next_epoch_before_steady` | protocol availability review | A backup INSTALLED in an epoch but still collecting EPOCH_STARTED promises accepts and commits the next RECONFIGURE from a primary that is already STEADY. The old core fenced it with `VSR_FAILURE_INVARIANT` at the commit notification; a later committed epoch necessarily follows the current handoff, so the backup now finishes that handoff locally, enters the next epoch, and the group reaches STEADY there |
| `api_init_out` | API conformance review | `vsr_init` must set `*out = NULL` on every error when `out` is valid; an arena overlapping the options record returned `EINVAL` before `out` was cleared |
| `pressure_reload` | `tests/fuzzy/cluster 137 1 2000 quiet 3` | A refused input at the minimum payload budget must not release the cache pin the next poll consumes (a witness's commit-notification head, reloaded before the retry); relief waits for an idle poll, so the notification finishes, announced pins are released, and the PREPARE is admitted |
| `lagging_reload` | `tests/fuzzy/cluster 1572 1 600 quiet 6` | A primary with two operation slots, one work unit per step and the minimum payload budget served two pairs of lagging peers while one slot stayed busy: a poll that ran while a peer's log load was outstanding rotated past that peer, failed the other pair's SEND, evicted its cached entry under the load's reservation, and resumed at the evicted pair, which reloaded its entry before the loaded one was ever sent (load, evict, reload, forever). The load's peer is now served first when the load completes, and a peer whose SEND lacks only an operation slot keeps the cursor |
| `anchor_behind_applied` | `tests/fuzzy/cluster 203 1 2000 quiet 3` | A full member whose application already reached a selected offer's checkpoint (the new primary picking a peer's longer DO_VIEW_CHANGE offer with a newer anchor than its own) must keep its state and fetch only the log past its position, not restore and install the anchor behind entries it applied |
| `reply_restore` | `tests/fuzzy/cluster 2 1 2000 quiet 3` | An EXECUTED reply decided from a client record loaded before a state transfer's RESTORE replaced the indexed client base must be decided again from the restored revision (and held while a restoration is outstanding), so every reply the host executes is backed by its readable store |
